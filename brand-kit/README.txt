FOUNDATION DENTAL CENTER — LOGO FILES

All SVG logos are extracted as actual vector geometry from the supplied PDFs. Lettering uses outlined glyph paths (no external fonts). No bitmap traces are used. SVG size can be scaled freely.

SVG files:

- fdc-logo-primary-dark.svg : gold arch and ivory text on slate background; with tagline
- fdc-logo-primary-dark-transparent.svg : gold arch and ivory text, transparent background, designed for dark placement
- fdc-logo-small-light.svg : gold arch and slate text, transparent background; without tagline
- fdc-logo-1color-black.svg : black logo and tagline, transparent background

PDF and EPS files are the ORIGINAL supplied vector files, included unmodified.
PNG previews at 2400 px wide are included for platforms that cannot use vector images.

Print: give your print shop the SVG, PDF, or EPS. Ask them to verify required CMYK/spot colors and production-specific specifications (cut vinyl, embroidery, etc.).
